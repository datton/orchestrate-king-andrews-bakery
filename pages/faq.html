Good to know

Questions, answered before you ask.

Ordering ahead, allergens, keeping your bakes fresh and eating the way you do.

The short answers

  • For celebration cakes and larger orders, use the cake enquiry: choose a size, flavour and message, and pick a pickup date with at least two days' notice. For everyday bakes, ask at the counter.

  • Two days is the minimum, and more is always kinder to the baker. The enquiry form will not offer dates sooner than that. Busy weekends fill first, so book early.

  • A bakery works with flour, milk, eggs, nuts and more, so please tell us about any allergy before you order and ask at the counter about a specific bake. Use the quick-check below to list what you avoid and show it when you order.

  • Keep bread in a paper bag or a bread bin at room temperature, not in the fridge, which dries it out. Eat pastries the day you buy them for the best texture. Anything with cream or custard belongs in the fridge.

  • A few minutes in a warm oven (around 160 °C) refreshes bread and pastries. Splash a little water on a crusty loaf first. A microwave makes them soft and chewy, so we avoid it.

  • Ask at the counter what is on the shelves today, as the selection changes. For a custom cake, mention your dietary needs in the enquiry notes and we will talk it through with you.
