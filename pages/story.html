Our story

The day starts before the street wakes up.

Andrew's Bakery is a neighbourhood bakery. We bake slowly, early, and for the people who live close enough to smell it.

What we believe

Good bread is not hurried. It is made of flour, water, salt and time, and the time is the part you cannot fake. So we give it all the time it asks for.

From dough to shelf

One loaf, three quiet stages.

Dough

1. Dough

Mixed in the small hours, then left to rise slowly. A long, patient rest is what gives the crumb its flavour.

2. Bake

The ovens are warm before dawn. We bake in small batches, so each tray comes out when it is ready, not when the clock says.

3. Shelf

Still warm when it reaches the counter. Whatever the morning holds, the shelves are filled with the day's bakes.

A bakery in small numbers.
